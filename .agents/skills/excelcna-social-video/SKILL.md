# ExcelCNA Social Video Intelligence

## Duration Decision (be smart, not fixed)

| Content Complexity | Duration | Scenes |
|---|---|---|
| Simple fact / single announcement | 30–45s | 4–6 |
| Single topic explainer | 45–65s | 6–9 |
| Multi-step process | 60–80s | 8–11 |
| Career story / detailed comparison | 70–90s | 10–14 |

**Pace rule:** ~2.5 words/second spoken. Each scene: 4–10 seconds. Decide total scenes from content complexity, not a fixed number.

---

## Image Type Selection — Be specific per scene

**Use `pexels` (real stock photography) when the scene shows:**
- People: caregivers, elderly patients, healthcare workers, nursing students, families
- Settings: home care environments, clinics, training classrooms, Pacific Northwest
- Emotions: trust, warmth, professional confidence, learning, compassion

**Use `ai_generated` (DALL-E infographic) when the scene shows:**
- Statistics, numbers, percentages (wages, job counts, enrollment stats)
- Step-by-step certification pathways or program timelines
- Price breakdowns ($95 down payment visualization)
- Comparison tables, checklists, credential badges
- Any data that needs custom illustration, not a photo of people

---

## Hook Formulas (proven for healthcare career content)

1. **Problem** — "Most people think starting a healthcare career takes years. It doesn't."
2. **Statistic** — "Washington State needs 40,000 more caregivers right now."
3. **Question** — "Looking for a healthcare job in Washington with no degree required?"
4. **Direct** — "Here's the clearest path to a caregiver job in Washington State."
5. **Contrast** — "You don't need 4 years. You need 75 hours."

## Scene Structure Templates

### Career Path Video (most common)
| Scene | Purpose | Image Type |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hook — job opportunity / stat | `ai_generated` (stat) or `pexels` (person) |
| 2 | Who this is for (audience) | `pexels` |
| 3–5 | Program details (how it works, timeline) | `pexels` + `ai_generated` mix |
| 6 | Credibility (DSHS approved, WA licensed) | `ai_generated` (badge/certification) |
| 7 | Easy entry / pricing | `ai_generated` (price infographic) |
| Last | CTA — go to ExcelCNA.com | `pexels` (confident person) |

### Educational Explainer
| Scene | Purpose | Image Type |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hook — surprising fact or question | `pexels` |
| 2–3 | Problem / context | `pexels` |
| 4–N-2 | Educational content points | `pexels` + `ai_generated` as needed |
| N-1 | Social proof / DSHS credentials | `pexels` or `ai_generated` |
| N | CTA | `pexels` |

## ExcelCNA Brand Facts (use specific numbers, not vague claims)

- **Program**: DSHS-approved HCA (Home Care Aide) training
- **Format**: Mostly online (some in-person clinical)
- **Duration**: 75 hours total
- **Entry cost**: $95 down payment to start
- **Location**: Des Moines, Washington (serves King + Pierce County)
- **HCA wages in WA**: $18–28/hour (use the range specifically)
- **CPR/BLS**: Certification from $40 (American Heart Association)
- **ACLS**: Available for experienced healthcare workers
- **Target audience**: Adults 22–55, career changers, family caregivers, re-entry workers

**Voice**: Plain, warm, practical. No hype. No corporate speak. Speak like a knowledgeable neighbor giving real advice. Use real prices, real hours, real county names.

High-performing queries for ExcelCNA content:
- `caregiver elderly patient home warmth`
- `healthcare worker smiling professional uniform`
- `nursing student certificate training classroom`
- `caregiver patient holding hands compassionate`
- `Washington state home care aide`
- `adult career change healthcare confident`
- `entry level medical job training`
- `CPR training certification medical`

Keep queries 3–5 words. Be specific. Avoid: "nurse" (wrong credential), "hospital" (wrong setting for HCA).

## AI Image Prompt Guidelines

For infographic/data scenes, always include:
- Background: white or light blue
- Style: "clean professional modern sans-serif typography"
- Color scheme: "healthcare blue and white"
- Be SPECIFIC about content: exact numbers, steps, labels

**Examples:**
- *Pathway*: "Clean infographic white background: 3-step HCA pathway — Enroll Online → Complete 75 Hours → Get DSHS Certificate. Blue checkmark icons, modern healthcare typography."
- *Salary*: "Minimalist infographic light blue background: Washington State HCA wages $18-28 per hour, upward arrow icon, dollar sign, professional healthcare design."
- *Price*: "Clean white background infographic: '$95 to start your HCA training' in large bold blue text, with healthcare cross icon and 'DSHS-Approved' badge."

## Platform Notes (brief)

| Platform | Format | Best Content | Duration Sweet Spot |
|---|---|---|---|
| TikTok | 9:16 vertical | Educational, fact-based, question hooks | 45–75s |
| Instagram Reels | 9:16 vertical | Professional, trust-building | 30–60s |
| YouTube Shorts | 9:16 vertical | Detailed explainers, searchable topics | 60–90s |

**Hashtag formula (5–7 tags):**
- 2 niche: `#HCAtraining` `#caregivercareersWA`
- 2 medium: `#healthcarecareers` `#washingtonstate`
- 1-2 broad: `#careerchange` `#learnontiktok`
- 1 branded: `#ExcelCNA`

**Hook quality check** — before finalizing, ask: "Will a Washington State adult 22–55 stop scrolling for this in the first 3 seconds?"

## Quality Checklist

- [ ] Hook works in first 3 seconds without sound
- [ ] Specific ExcelCNA facts used (not generic)
- [ ] Image types match content (pexels for people, AI for data)
- [ ] Scene count matches content complexity (not over-padded)
- [ ] Clear single CTA at the end
- [ ] Total duration matches complexity of content (30–90s range)
- [ ] Text overlays are 2–6 words maximum
- [ ] Brand voice: plain, warm, practical — no hype
